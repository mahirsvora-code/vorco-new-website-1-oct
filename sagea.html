Bringing SAGEA's AI capabilities to India

Connecting enterprise problems with technology.

VORCO Solutions has partnered with SAGEA Labs Pvt. Ltd., a Nepal-based AI research and technology company, to support SAGEA's entry into the Indian market.

VORCO will focus on developing enterprise relationships, identifying opportunities, understanding local business requirements and supporting commercial engagement in India. SAGEA will remain responsible for the underlying technology, technical evaluation, engineering and deployment of its AI systems.

The initial focus will be on financial services and other data-intensive industries where AI can be applied to operational workflows involving identity, documents, enterprise information, customer interaction and automation.

From Nepal to India

A local path into a new enterprise market.

India is SAGEA's first market outside Nepal. For VORCO, the partnership connects Indian organisations with specialised AI capabilities while helping SAGEA understand the requirements of a new market.

The partnership is focused on practical production environments: existing infrastructure, data, workflows, security requirements and operational processes.

The next stage

The VORCO–SAGEA partnership establishes a foundation for both organisations to work with enterprises across India. As engagements develop, the partnership will explore additional industries and use cases where SAGEA's technology can address practical business and operational requirements.

Together, VORCO and SAGEA are building a practical path for enterprise AI in India.
